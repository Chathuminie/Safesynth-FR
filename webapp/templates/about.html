About SafeSynth-FR

Privacy-first facial recognition with synthetic data

🛡️ What is SafeSynth-FR?

A privacy-preserving face recognition system trained entirely on synthetic facial data. No real biometric information is processed or stored.

🔒 Privacy with Synthetic Data

By using synthetic faces, we ensure zero exposure of real biometric data. The model learns facial patterns without compromising individual privacy.

🧠 Model & Pipeline

Powered by MobileNetV2 deep learning architecture. Optimized for real-time inference on modern devices while maintaining high accuracy.

🧪 Robustness Testing

Tested across 30 identities with variations in lighting conditions, viewing angles, and facial masks to ensure reliable performance.

📈 Key Results

High accuracy on masked and unmasked faces. Robust recognition even under challenging environmental conditions and extreme angles.

🚀 Future Work

Expanding to additional identities, improving real-time performance, and exploring federated learning for edge deployment.

Technical Details

Architecture

  • 🔹 MobileNetV2 backbone
  • 🔹 224x224 input resolution
  • 🔹 30 identity classes
  • 🔹 Softmax + threshold decision

Training Data

  • 🔹 Synthetic face generation
  • 🔹 Data augmentation applied
  • 🔹 Masked variants included
  • 🔹 Split: 70/15/15 (train/val/test)

Performance

  • 🔹 High accuracy rates
  • 🔹 Real-time inference
  • 🔹 Mask-robust recognition
  • 🔹 Cross-lighting robustness

FAQ

Q: Is my data stored?

A: No. SafeSynth-FR processes images in real-time without storing raw biometric data. Only model predictions are logged.

Q: How accurate is the model?

A: The model achieves high accuracy across 30 identities. Performance varies based on image quality, lighting, and pose angle.

Q: Does it work with masks?

A: Yes! The model was trained with masked face variations and handles partial facial occlusion robustly.

Q: What if a face isn't recognized?

A: Unknown faces are labeled as "Unknown Person" with a low confidence score, indicating the face doesn't match any trained identity.
